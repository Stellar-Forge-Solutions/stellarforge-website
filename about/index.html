About Stellar Forge

An independent engineering studio, run by one engineer

Stellar Forge is a freelance technology engineering practice — not an agency with layers of account managers. When you work with Stellar Forge, you work directly with the person designing and building your system.

Background

Stellar Forge is built on 5–6 years of hands-on experience across AWS, FinOps, cloud infrastructure, and the wider engineering stack — from backend architecture and APIs to authentication, security, and edge computing on Cloudflare.

That experience shapes how projects are scoped: infrastructure decisions are made with real cost and operational impact in mind, not just technical novelty. Security and observability are built in from the start rather than retrofitted after launch.

How engagements work

  • You work directly with the engineer building your system — no handoffs between sales and delivery
  • Projects are scoped around your actual constraints: budget, timeline, and team
  • Systems are documented and handed over cleanly, so nothing is a black box
  • Ongoing support and iteration are available after launch, not just a one-time delivery
Expertise

Cloud & Edge

AI & Automation

SaaS & Platforms

Multi-tenant SaaS platforms, connected product ecosystems, authentication and SSO, billing, and admin tooling.
