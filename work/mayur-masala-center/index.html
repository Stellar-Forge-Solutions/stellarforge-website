The problem

Mayur Masala Center, a spice retailer, needed to sell online and needed billing software that matched how their physical shop actually runs — scanning products by barcode at the counter — rather than a generic point-of-sale tool built for a different kind of retail business. They also needed a simple, reliable way to track business expenses alongside sales.

Services applied

High-Performance Web AppsSaaS PlatformsAutomation & Integrations

What we built

  • Built a custom e-commerce storefront tailored to how the business catalogs and sells its products online.
  • Developed a billing application with barcode generation and scanning, so in-store checkout matches the shop's existing physical workflow instead of forcing a new process onto staff.
  • Added expense management directly into the platform, so the business can track outgoings alongside sales in one place rather than a separate spreadsheet.
  • Designed the system so the billing and e-commerce sides share a consistent product catalog, reducing duplicate data entry.

The outcome

Mayur Masala Center now runs both its online sales and in-store billing from a system built around its actual day-to-day operations — barcode scanning at the counter, a proper online storefront, and expense tracking in one place, rather than three disconnected tools.
