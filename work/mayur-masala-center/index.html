Retail (Spices & Consumer Goods)

Mayur Masala Center

Stellar Forge built a connected set of retail operations tools for Mayur Masala Center — combining a customer-facing e-commerce platform with the billing, barcode, and back-office systems the business runs on internally.

Scope
E-commerce platform, billing & barcode systems, internal operations tools
Client site
mayurmasalacenter.in

The challenge

As a retail spice business, Mayur Masala Center needed to sell online with proper billing and print-ready invoicing, track inventory at the point of sale with barcodes, and manage day-to-day operations — expenses and employee records — without relying on disconnected spreadsheets and manual processes.

What we built

E-commerce platform

Built a customer-facing e-commerce site with integrated, print-ready billing, so orders placed online generate accurate invoices without manual re-entry.

Barcode & billing system

Delivered a point-of-sale-style barcode and billing system for in-store and order processing, keeping product and pricing data consistent across online and offline sales.

Expense management

Built an expense management application to track and categorize business costs, replacing manual spreadsheet tracking with a structured system.

Employee management

Delivered an employee management platform to handle staff records and operational tracking as part of the same connected system.

Technology

E-commerce platformBilling & invoicingBarcode systemsExpense trackingEmployee management

Outcome

Mayur Masala Center now runs online sales, in-store billing, and internal operations (expenses and staff) through one connected set of tools instead of disconnected manual processes.
