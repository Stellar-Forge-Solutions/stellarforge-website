The challenge

Stellar Global Supplies needed to run its entire business — customer orders, quoting, billing, marketing, and internal operations — without duct-taping together outside SaaS tools, while keeping infrastructure costs low and the whole thing secure enough to trust with real customer data at real scale. That meant building custom, purpose-fit software for nearly every function of the business, on infrastructure that could scale globally without a large hosting bill.

The platform

16 products, one connected system

Single sign-on ties every app together — one login gives the team access to the whole suite. Below: the customer- and business-facing products first, followed by the security, monitoring, and infrastructure layer running underneath them.
