The problem

Snehal Printers, a printing business, relied on manual outreach to find new print work — a slow, inconsistent process that depended entirely on however much time the team had that week. They also needed a proper online storefront to give the business a digital presence beyond word-of-mouth and manual orders.

Services applied

AI-Powered ApplicationsHigh-Performance Web AppsAutomation & Integrations

What we built

  • Built an AI-powered lead generation system that scrapes and identifies potential leads relevant to the business.
  • Automated the first draft of outreach emails to those leads, so the team reviews and sends rather than writing from scratch each time.
  • Built a custom e-commerce website so customers can browse and order print products online directly.
  • Applied the same AI lead-generation approach used for Stellar Global Supplies, adapted to the print industry's products and customer base.

The outcome

Snehal Printers now has a repeatable, automated way to find and reach new print work, alongside a proper e-commerce storefront — giving the business both a growth engine and a digital front door it didn't have before.
