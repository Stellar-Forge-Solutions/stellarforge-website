Snehal Printers

Stellar Forge built Snehal Printers' business website and a complementary AI-powered lead generation platform to help the business find and reach new customers beyond word-of-mouth and walk-in traffic.

Scope
Business website & AI-driven lead generation platform
Client site
snehalprinters.in

The challenge

As a local printing business, Snehal Printers needed a professional web presence to be found and evaluated by new customers, along with a way to proactively identify potential leads rather than relying solely on inbound and referral traffic.

What we built

Business website

Designed and built a fast, mobile-friendly business website presenting Snehal Printers' services clearly, with a focus on load speed and straightforward enquiry paths for potential customers.

AI-powered lead generation

Built an automated system that uses AI-powered scraping to identify and qualify potential leads relevant to the printing business, reducing time spent on manual prospecting.

Technology

Web developmentAI-powered scrapingLead generation automation

Outcome

Snehal Printers gained both a professional online presence and an ongoing, automated way to source new leads rather than depending on manual outreach alone.
