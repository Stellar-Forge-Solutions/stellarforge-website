How we work

A process built for clarity, not ceremony

01

Discover

We start by understanding the actual problem — your data, constraints, and what "done" needs to mean — before proposing an architecture.

02

Architect

System design, data modeling, and technology choices are made deliberately and documented, so decisions are visible and reviewable.

03

Build

Iterative delivery with working software early — so you're validating direction in weeks, not waiting months for a single reveal.

04

Ship

Deployment, monitoring, and security are part of the build, not an afterthought bolted on before launch.

05

Support

Many engagements continue post-launch — as ongoing engineering support, new features, or fractional technical advisory.

Why Stellar Forge

Direct access to the engineer who builds your system

No account managers relaying requirements between you and the people writing code. You work directly with the engineer designing and building your system — with 5–6 years of hands-on AWS, FinOps, and full-stack experience behind every decision.

Security-first by default

Authentication, access control, and secrets handling built correctly from the start, not patched in before launch.

Edge-native where it counts

Real production experience migrating and building on Cloudflare's edge platform — not just familiarity with the docs.

Observability built in

Systems shipped with logging, monitoring, and alerting from day one — so problems are visible before customers report them.
