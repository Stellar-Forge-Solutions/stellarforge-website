Product Engineering

SaaS platforms engineered to scale, not just launch

Shipping a SaaS MVP is the easy part. The hard part is the multi-tenant data model, the billing edge cases, the admin tooling your team will live in daily, and the architecture that doesn't need a rewrite the moment you get real customers. Stellar Forge builds for that second stage from day one.

What breaks as SaaS products grow

Products built for a demo rarely survive their first hundred paying customers without pain: tenant data isolation gets bolted on late, billing logic multiplies into unmanageable edge cases, and there's no internal tooling for support or operations — so every issue becomes an engineering fire drill.

Our approach

Multi-tenant from the start

Data isolation, role-based access, and account hierarchies designed before the first customer, not after the tenth.

Billing that handles reality

Usage-based, tiered, or seat-based billing logic that accounts for upgrades, downgrades, trials, and failed payments.

Operational tooling included

Internal dashboards for support, usage analytics, and account management — so your team isn't debugging in a database console.

Architecture that scales sideways

Services and data models built to add features without destabilizing what already works.

What's included

  • Multi-tenant SaaS architecture and data modeling
  • Subscription billing and usage metering integration
  • Admin dashboards, analytics, and internal operations tooling
  • Onboarding flows, role-based permissions, and account management
  • API design for customer-facing and internal integrations
