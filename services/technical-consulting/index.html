Consulting

A senior engineer's perspective, before you commit to a direction

Some of the most expensive mistakes in software happen at the decision stage — the wrong architecture, the wrong vendor, the wrong build-vs-buy call — long before a line of code proves it wrong. Stellar Forge offers direct, hands-on technical consulting to help you make that decision with confidence.

When outside technical judgment helps most

Founders and small teams often don't have a senior engineer in the room for high-stakes technical decisions — evaluating a vendor, deciding whether to rebuild or extend a system, or preparing for a technical due-diligence conversation with an investor or acquirer. Getting it wrong is expensive; getting a second opinion usually isn't.

Our approach

Architecture and technology reviews

An honest assessment of your current system's strengths, risks, and scaling limits — with concrete recommendations.

Build-vs-buy and vendor evaluation

Structured comparison of the real options, including total cost and long-term maintenance burden, not just feature checklists.

Technical due diligence support

Codebase and infrastructure review to support fundraising, acquisition, or partnership conversations.

Fractional senior engineering input

Ongoing or project-based access to senior technical judgment without a full-time hire.

What's included

  • Architecture and codebase review with a written report
  • Build-vs-buy and technology stack evaluation
  • Technical due diligence for fundraising or M&A
  • Fractional CTO-style advisory for early-stage teams
  • Roadmap and technical hiring guidance
