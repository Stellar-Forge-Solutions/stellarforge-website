FAQ

Common questions

Is this a one-time engagement or ongoing?

Both are available — a one-time architecture review, or ongoing advisory as your team makes recurring technical decisions.

Do you also implement the recommendations?

Yes, if you'd like — many consulting engagements naturally continue into the build itself, but there's no obligation to.

Who is this for?

Founders and small teams making a significant technical decision — a migration, a rebuild, or a first hire into a technical role — who want an independent perspective first.
